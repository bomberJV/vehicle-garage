## G500 schematic geometry — November 13, 2026

- Mercedes-Benz, *G-Class Operator's Manual, USA Edition A 2002*, order 6515 4039 13 / part 463 584 12 83, technical-data pages 359–360. Primary manufacturer document available through a [dealer-manual mirror](https://cdn.dealereprocess.org/cdn/servicemanuals/mercedesbenz/2002-gclass.pdf). Indexed text was available for this revision; direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403. The indexed table specifies a 2,850 mm wheelbase, 1,515 mm front/rear track, 265/60 R18 tires, and 7½J × 18 H2 ET43 rims. The model uses half-track 0.7575 m, nominal rim radius 0.2286 m, and nominal unloaded tire radius 0.3876 m, derived as `(18 × 25.4 / 2 + 265 × 0.60) / 1000`. Tire deflection, actual rim-flange profiles, offsets at individual mounting surfaces, and production tolerances are not modeled.
- Mercedes-Benz Media, [*Stronger than time: Mercedes-Benz G-Class ready for the age of e-mobility*](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/c0dfbd7d-7753-47be-8771-aacbb5bafd05), September 5, 2021. Its historical design discussion identifies the continuing round-headlamp, prominent front-indicator, exterior-protection-strip and rear-spare-wheel features. It supports the family design language; it does not establish 2002 component dimensions or lighting part numbers.
- The original procedural schematic now includes separate circular G500 headlamp lens, reflector, housing, bonnet-top indicator, wheel tire/rim/hub/brake disc/caliper, frame, solid axle, steering linkage, coil spring, damper, engine, intake and radiator meshes. Their detailed shapes and exploded spacing are explanatory approximations. This is not manufacturer CAD, a complete parts catalog, a dimensioned assembly drawing or a service procedure. No exact replacement-lighting part number is inferred from the current online Mercedes catalog: its year-filtered listings can include later designs.
