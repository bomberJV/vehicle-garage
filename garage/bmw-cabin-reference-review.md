# 2003 325Ci coupé cabin, rear vent windows and service placement

Reviewed 14 November 2026. Target: US-market pre-facelift 2003 E46 **M54** coupé. The convertible’s vertically retracting rear windows and the M56 SULEV fuel system are different assemblies.

## Material actually inspected

- Downloaded and rendered BMW's [2003 coupé owner’s manual, 01 41 0 156 800, ©09/02 BMW AG](https://bmw330ci.net/the330ci/2003Manual-3SeriesCoupe.pdf). Printed p. 40 shows the four window switches beside the gear lever and explicitly distinguishes the outward-moving tilt-out panes. It permits automatic opening but says the tilt-out panes do not automatically reclose. Printed p. 140 shows the fuse carrier behind the glovebox panel and its two clips. These pages were visually inspected, not inferred from search snippets.
- Visually inspected the [2003 North American BMW coupé brochure](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/2003-BMW-3-Series-Coupe-CN.pdf), PDF p. 5: black interior, contoured front seats, separate head restraints, three-spoke wheel, E46 binnacle, centre stack, tunnel controls, armrest, and long inner door cards. This is the Canadian-market brochure; it is used for shared LHD cabin shapes, not to assign US options or specifications. Its pre-facelift photographs corroborate the body style and window proportions.
- Visually inspected both drawings in the [2003 M54 coupé window-lift catalog](https://www.bmwpartsdeal.com/parts-list/2003-bmw-325ci-coupe_m54_engine_e46/vehicle_trim/door_window_lifting_mechanism.html), especially rear vent-window plate **51_3687**, drawing stamp 00157853. It separates glass, front fittings, lower railing gasket, inner seal, handed rear drives (67158238743/744), O-rings, inner/outer caps, two-pole connector and contacts. The [same BMW plate via RealOEM](https://www.realoem.com/bmw/enUS/showparts?diagId=51_3687&id=BD31-EUR-08_2002_E46_BMW_325Ci) corroborates these part identities; its EUR selection is not used to choose US equipment.
- Visually inspected [M54 tank/attaching-parts](https://www.bmwpartsdeal.com/parts-list/2003-bmw-325ci-coupe_m54_engine_e46/fuel_system/fuel_tank_attaching_parts.html) and [fuel pump/level-sensor drawings](https://www.bmwpartsdeal.com/parts-list/2003-bmw-325ci-coupe_m54_engine_e46/fuel_system/fuel_pump_and_fuel_level_sensor.html), the latter stamp 00208623. The list identifies right delivery unit 16146766942, separate left sensor 16116768788, suction jet assembly 16146755880, paired flange seals, locking rings, and separate strainer. Checked the [battery tray catalog](https://www.bmwpartsdeal.com/parts-list/2003-bmw-325ci-coupe_m54_engine_e46/vehicle_trim/battery_tray.html), including the luggage-compartment battery application.
- The [US 2003 325Ci auction](https://carsandbids.com/auctions/3LY4gWBR/2003-bmw-325ci-coupe) confirms that its photographed example is an M54 coupé, but its image host blocked local inspection. Its photographs are therefore **not** claimed as an inspected geometry reference.

## Implementation

`bmw-cabin-closures.js` replaces the generic cabin with contoured cushions/backrests/head restraints, separate front seat instances, rear bench, an E46 cockpit, four window switches, handbrake, cupholders, long door cards and rear shelf. The cabin floor has a formed gearbox/shaft tunnel instead of a slab intersecting the driveline. The tunnel was checked against the installed gearbox and shaft by the running-gear agent. Behind the front-seat rails, the rear floor steps over the tank and its service flanges. The old excessively deep rear-seat upholstery block was thinned at its underside; the cushion top and upper head-restraint positions were retained. The rear console follows the raised tunnel rather than extending through its skin.

The rear quarter panes use the exterior agent’s final world-space outlines. Both forward fittings stay fixed on the sloping leading edge while the rear swings outward. A constant-length articulated external arm joins the fixed drive to the moving glass fitting. The whole-vehicle selector uses `bmwVentPosition`; the independently animated studies open through `body.quarter-left` and `body.quarter-right`. The details expose glass, fixed fittings, seal, drive, arm, glass fitting, rings, caps and connector separately.

`bmw-fuel-system.js` supplies an M54 saddle-tank section with both level units, the right electric pump, separate suction-jet transfer assembly, seals, right filler connection and supporting bands. The installed right pump and left sender were already on the correct sides. The pump was raised slightly to remain inside the tank; the strainer and transfer hose were added. The filler neck now reaches the actual right-quarter flap around x = 1.64 m, instead of ending ahead of the rear axle at x = 1.19 m. The glovebox fuse carrier now sits vertically behind the dashboard, replacing a low carrier at x = −0.01 m in the front-seat area. The right-rear luggage battery location was retained.

## Limits and checks

These are reconstructed surfaces, not factory CAD. The vent opening angle (0.075 rad), external arm dimensions, sealed drive shape, cabin dimensions, trim and hose routes are illustrative. Exact internal gears/cams, window sealing loads, manufacturing tolerances, restraint internals and complete wiring are omitted. The cabin depicts black trim; it does not assert the car’s original upholstery, seat options or transmission. Fuel transfer topology is represented; pressure regulation, evap plumbing and a calibrated fluid simulation are not.

Targeted command: `node --test tests/garage-bmw-cabin.test.mjs tests/garage-road-service.test.mjs tests/garage-e46-running-gear.test.mjs`. At review: **12 passed**. Checks include fixed vent pivots, preserved pane shape, constant arm lengths, live whole-car selector/cache invalidation, selectable hardware, finite geometry, correct fuel sides/filler location, glovebox carrier, and clearance above the actual installed gearbox/shaft.
