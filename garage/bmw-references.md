The model represents a U.S. model-year 2003 E46 325Ci coupe with the M54B25 engine. The original windshield rake, two-door greenhouse, amber front corners, paired headlamps and early rear lamps distinguish it from the later coupe.

The mesh is original explanatory geometry, not licensed BMW CAD, and does not encode factory manufacturing tolerances or replacement-panel compatibility.

Legacy E90 build migration changes vehicle identity and retains appearance, notes and budget. It deliberately clears all selected products, quotes, labor and paint allowances so an old N52 filter or E90 tire quote cannot silently become E46 fitment. New BMW catalog lines use b46-prefixed IDs.

The frameless front glass and door mirror move with each long door. The rear quarter windows pivot outward at their forward mounts. The body carries the sills and fixed quarter panels. The kidneys move with the hood, and the inner rear lamps move with the luggage lid. The stock wheel is the 7J × 16 Style 43 with 47 mm offset and a 205/55 R16 tire. Wheel options varied by car.

### M54B25 intake, crankcase ventilation and cam phasing

BMW's June 2000 ST036 training drawings show two groups of three runners, a vacuum-operated crossover flap, the electronic throttle and a separate idle-air branch. The DISA component study opens through the same engine leaf that appears in the installed view. Its cutaway follows BMW's photograph on p. 42 and shows the shaft, lever, actuator and spring in the long-path and open-crossover states. Pages 32–33 describe the common double-VANOS assembly, independent solenoids and the helical action that converts each piston's travel to cam rotation.
