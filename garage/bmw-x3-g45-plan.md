# Plan · 2025 BMW X3 30 xDrive (G45)

Built with the [new-vehicle procedure](NEW-VEHICLE.md). Status: **building**. The body outline is traced (below); the vehicle is not in the fleet yet.

## Name

BMW sells no "X3 330i". From the 2025 model year BMW dropped the "i" from combustion model names, so the US four-cylinder X3 is the **X3 30 xDrive**; the only other US version is the six-cylinder M50 xDrive. ([BMW Canada press release, June 2024](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/canada/article/attachment/T0443201EN/618394); the [US release](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/usa/article/detail/T0443207EN_US) lists the 2025 X3 30 xDrive at $49,500 and the M50 xDrive at $64,100)

**Closure check:** 880 + 2,865 + 1,010 = 4,755 mm. The overhangs close with the published length.

**Track:** the three published track pairs differ by up to 23 mm per axle. Use the US 30 xDrive pair, 63.5 / 65.3 in, which belongs to the standard 19-inch car (confirm against the NCAP test car's measurements); do not average.

**Wheel radius:** 245/50 R19: (19 × 25.4 / 2 + 245 × 0.50) / 1000 = 0.3638 m.

**Frame:** axles at x = ∓1.4325 m; stock tyre contact z = 0.040.

## Phase 3 · Body: what to get right

- **Proportions:** 880 mm front overhang, 1,010 mm rear overhang, 1,660 mm height. Register everything at the two wheel centres.
- **Grille:** the large kidney with vertical and diagonal bars; no Iconic Glow.
- **Lamps:** headlamp and tail-lamp outlines and graphics from photographs of US cars.
- **Glasshouse:** front door, rear door and fixed quarter glass outlines; the Hofmeister kink; the D-pillar.
- **Bumpers:** traced for the decided package only.
- **Sections:** at least ten, checked against the 1,920 mm body width and the rescue-sheet plan view.
- **Panels:** bumper covers, wings, bonnet, four doors, tailgate, sills, roof and the panoramic moonroof.

## Studies

1. Belt-drive starter-generator: how it starts the engine, recovers energy and powers the 48 V circuit.
2. xDrive transfer case: how drive is split between the rear axle and the front shaft.
3. B48 valvetrain and VANOS (shared with the X1).
