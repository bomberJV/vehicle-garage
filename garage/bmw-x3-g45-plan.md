# Plan · 2026 BMW X3 30 xDrive (G45)

Built with the [new-vehicle procedure](NEW-VEHICLE.md). Status: **planning**. Nothing below is modelled yet.

## Name

BMW sells no "X3 330i". From the 2025 model year BMW dropped the "i" from combustion model names, so the US four-cylinder X3 is the **X3 30 xDrive**; the only other US version is the six-cylinder M50 xDrive. ([BMW Canada press release, June 2024](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/canada/article/attachment/T0443201EN/618394), [cars.com 2026 trims](https://www.cars.com/research/bmw-x3-2026/specs/478363/))

| Wheel package | **Decide.** Standard 19-inch; 20- and 21-inch packages are optional | BMW US press release |

**Recommendation:** M Sport Package on the standard 19-inch wheel. Most US cars carry the M Sport body, and the standard wheel keeps the track figures unambiguous. Add the 20- and 21-inch wheels as selectable options, stock as the default.

**Closure check:** 880 + 2,865 + 1,010 = 4,755 mm. The overhangs close with the published length.

**Track:** the three published track pairs differ by up to 23 mm per axle. Take the pair BMW publishes for the decided wheel and tyre; do not average them. Get the 19-inch tyre size from BMW's US technical data or a door placard, not a reseller page.

**Frame:** axles at x = ∓1.4325 m; stock tyre contact z = 0.040.

## Phase 3 · Body: what to get right

- **Proportions:** 880 mm front overhang, 1,010 mm rear overhang, 1,660 mm height. Register everything at the two wheel centres.
- **Grille:** the large kidney with diagonal bars, and, if chosen, the Iconic Glow contour light around each kidney.
- **Lamps:** headlamp and tail-lamp outlines and graphics from photographs of US cars.
- **Glasshouse:** front door, rear door and fixed quarter glass outlines; the Hofmeister kink; the D-pillar.
- **Bumpers:** traced for the decided package only.
- **Sections:** at least ten, checked against the 1,920 mm body width and the rescue-sheet plan view.
- **Panels:** bumper covers, wings, bonnet, four doors, tailgate, sills, roof, and the moonroof if chosen.

## Studies

1. Belt-drive starter-generator: how it starts the engine, recovers energy and powers the 48 V circuit.
2. xDrive transfer case: how drive is split between the rear axle and the front shaft.
3. B48 valvetrain and VANOS (shared with the X1).
