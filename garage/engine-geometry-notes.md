# Engine component mesh notes

The engine mesh is an original assembly study. It exposes separate rotating, valvetrain, air/fuel, ignition, lubrication, cooling, accessory, electrical and exhaust components. Its cylinders, camshaft counts, valve counts and spark-plug counts follow the architecture in `engine-data.js`. It does not provide production casting dimensions, gasket thicknesses, service clearances, a measured hose route, firing/timing profiles, torque values or an installation/removal sequence. Small features are enlarged for inspection.

The module distinguishes the longitudinal M113 V8, longitudinal M54 inline-six, longitudinal M156 V8 and transverse US HR-V inline-four/CVT relationship. Ford engine internals remain unselected: only a configuration boundary is drawn, avoiding an invented gas or diesel engine.

The wheel-independent engine mesh includes open cylinder liners, crankshaft journals/throws, rods, piston bodies and rings, main bearings, gaskets, head castings/ports, valve stems, camshafts, valve covers, timing-drive relationships, sump/pickup/pump/filter, manifold runners, throttle, fuel rail/injectors, coils/plugs, exhaust branches, radiator fins/fan blades, hoses, reservoirs, accessories and belt path. These shapes communicate function and connection. The cam lobes are representative contact shapes, not a verified production lobe profile or timing simulation. M113 uses three separate rocker designs (two intake and one exhaust) per the OE supplier bulletin; its exact cam lobe geometry has not been measured.

Additional visual/source checks:

- **2002 G500 engine bay photograph:** [RM Sotheby's original vehicle listing](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/az23/lots/r0041-2002-mercedesbenz-g-500/), [photograph](https://cdn.rmsothebys.com/c/7/9/b/7/3/c79b7359c5a3a4254fff352a54accb606f862a23.webp). This was used for the recognizable overhead housing/cover, paired front intake ducts and broad bay arrangement. It is a vehicle photograph, not a dimensioned technical drawing.
- **M113 valve-actuation hardware:** [Schaeffler INA 0039 service information](https://vehiclelifetimesolutions.schaeffler.fi/medias/INA-0039-en.PDF), original OE supplier publication distinguishing Intake1, Intake2 and exhaust rocker designs. This supports three separate rocker arrangements, not detailed cam timing/profile.
- **G500 battery location:** Mercedes-Benz 2002 G-Class operator manual, printed pp.319–321 ([primary document reprint](https://cdn.dealereprocess.org/cdn/servicemanuals/mercedesbenz/2002-gclass.pdf)). Its battery is below the cup holder ahead of the rear seat bench, with jump terminals in the left engine compartment. Accordingly, the bay model shows a remote terminal rather than a battery box.
- **BMW M54:** [BMW technical training, June2000](https://bmwtuning.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/BMW-M54-Training-Doc.pdf), primary manufacturer document reprint, plus the vehicle references in engine-data.js. The bay power interface represents the E46 remote jump-start terminal; its battery is in the luggage compartment.
- **M156 air connections:** Daimler XENTRY technical bulletin [LI07.61-P-048993, July2014](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2014/SB-10062776-2280.pdf) identifies air-filter branches/intake manifold and several related sealing interfaces. The visible duct paths are explanatory, not the measured molded production routes.
- **US 2026 HR-V:** [Honda specifications](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-6f8b202ddb1c7f26ecd92774bb014b0c-2026-honda-hr-v-specifications-and-features) establish the 2.0L DOHC16valve, multi-point port-injected inline-four. This is not the Brazilian/Japanese market engine bay.

`buildEngineBay(renderer, definition, {detail})` emits all detailed leaves only in the engine inspection view. With detail false it emits no faces, keeping a hidden coarse proxy from protruding through a closed hood. Each face carries a leaf tag for picking. `enginePartOffset` supplies a rigid per-component separation vector; separation is an inspection arrangement, not a removal path. The full detailed engine mesh stays under10000 faces per supported engine to limit software-rendering cost.

Tests in `tests/garage-engine-geometry.test.mjs` check every listed engine leaf against emitted geometry, finite vertices/colors, face budget, absence of a closed-hood proxy, actual piston/plug mesh instance counts, longitudinal/transverse cylinder arrangement, remote electrical-interface dimensions and distinct head/cover/sump separation.


### 2002 US G500, M113.962

The crank axis follows the axle-to-axle centerline. The radiator, viscous fan and belt sit ahead of the engine and the bellhousing sits behind it. The fitted air cleaner has two side housings, a crowned center cover and two front corrugated inlets. The common outlet at the rear connects the hot-film meter to the throttle. The oil dipstick loop sits forward on the passenger bank. The filler cap and filter housing are distinct on the driver's side. The coolant tank follows the right inner fender and the brake reservoir sits toward the left bulkhead. Scale and positions come from comparison with the front-of-vehicle photograph. [2002 G500 photograph](https://cdn.rmsothebys.com/c/7/9/b/7/3/c79b7359c5a3a4254fff352a54accb606f862a23.webp) · [Matching vehicle](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/az23/lots/r0041-2002-mercedesbenz-g-500/) · [2002 G500 air-cleaner illustration, items 1–6](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/parts-list/2002-mercedes_benz-g500/air-fuel-delivery/air-intake).

Mercedes service document AR01.30-P-5800GV covers 113.962 in the 463.249. Plate P01.30-2025-06 shows the overhead cleaner, air-meter tube and front fan. P01.30-2026-09 places the ignition coils on the sides of the cylinder-head covers. Mercedes labels the pictured engine 112.945, so its cylinder count and outline were not used for the V8. The G500 mesh uses four dual-output coil units on each bank. Both leads meet separate inclined plug bodies. Housing contours, individual hose paths, boots and relative heights are illustrative.
