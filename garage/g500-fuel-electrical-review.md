# 2002 G500 fuel and electrical placement review

Reviewed 14 November 2026. Vehicle coordinates are schematic; source drawings establish locations, sides and assembly relationships, not production dimensions.

- Read the 2002 US operator's manual, printed [p. 319](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/118401/mercedes-benz-2002-g-class-2002-g-500-g-class-2002-owners-manual.html?page=322): the battery is in front of the rear bench below the cup holder; the left engine bay contains remote jump terminals. Visually inspected the original [Mercedes-Benz rescue card](https://rk.mb-qr.com/en/463/) top and side drawings, using its **as of 12/2000** battery branch. Its older under-hood and cargo-side battery symbols describe other build periods and are not fitted simultaneously.
- Read the US manual printed pp. 297–300 via [the page-specific manual reprint](https://www.manualowl.com/am/Mercedes/2002-G-Class/Manual/1379?page=300): the four access locations are the driver dashboard end, front passenger footwell, middle tunnel next to the passenger seat, and battery box. The text describes two footwell covers and a folding carrier. It does not establish exact bracket angles here; the simplified flat carriers remain illustrative. Manual drawing downloads were blocked, so those drawings were not used for geometric measurements.
- Checked the [Mercedes-Benz Classic 2002 fuel-system plate](https://classicparts.mbusa.com/v-2002-mercedes-benz-g500--base--5-0l-v8-gas/fuel-system--fuel-system-components) and visually inspected its [OEM exploded drawing on the catalog mirror](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/resources/encry/diagram/motor/2025/medium/c3c60d3de44cf67999c7df0755a79872.png). It distinguishes the tank, paired supporting straps/handed sleeves, separate shield, sender, external pump with clamp/insulator, separate filter, and retainer plate. The rescue-card drawing independently places the tank behind the rear axle. Filler access remains on the right rear quarter.

## Concrete corrections

1. Added the missing physical battery below the rear cup holder, including case, tray, top posts, short positive lead to the adjacent fuse block and negative body bond. The earlier battery-box block alone did not represent a physical battery. Moved the block immediately forward so the two solid bodies do not overlap. The under-hood object remains a remote power interface.
2. Kept the four fuse locations, corrected the driver's carrier footprint and moved it inward. Its old center/half-width extended to y = −0.932 m, outside the body. The revised footprint remains inside y = −0.821 m. The tunnel carrier remains on the passenger side.
3. Retained the tank and external pump/filter behind the axle. Added the previously absent delivery bracket, clamps and port stubs, plus short hoses that physically join tank-to-pump and pump-to-filter. A short forward outlet marks the feed interface; hidden full-length supply/return/evap routes are not guessed.
4. Changed the paired tank bands and their liners to left/right longitudinal supports in the installed and section views, consistent with the paired straps/handed sleeves and guard drawing.

The battery well is beneath the floor covering and is visible in the electrical assembly cutaway. Full harness routing, fuse assignments, fuel-pressure regulation, evap hardware, exact hose routing and manufacturing clearances remain outside this model. The filter connection shows physical continuity, not a certified port map or service instruction.
