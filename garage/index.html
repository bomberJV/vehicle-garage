How this garage works

Model references

Open References to compare a model with its source drawings. The G500 combines W463 parts diagrams with original 3D geometry. Component locations and line routes are schematic. The factory dimension view compares the exterior measurements.

The Ford drawing shows a regular cab and a long bed. Engine options and tire sizes varied, so its supplier links let you look up the equipment on a particular truck.

Prices and fitment

Every catalog item links to the manufacturer, retailer or service provider used in the research. Catalog prices are in US dollars and reflect the date shown. A quote you enter replaces the entire line total, including every tire in that line.

A selected catalog line is a planning entry. Check the vehicle identification number, equipment and supplier fitment before ordering. The source download includes the parts catalog and budget calculations.
