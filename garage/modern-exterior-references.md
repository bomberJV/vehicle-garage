# C63 and HR-V exterior geometry references

Checked November 13, 2026. These are manufacturer-authored sources. No external mesh, texture or image was copied into the project; the module contains original explanatory geometry.

## 2014 Mercedes-Benz C63 AMG sedan

- Mercedes-Benz USA, *2014 C-Class Sedan/Coupe* brochure, pages 3, 13–14 and 28: https://cdn.dealereprocess.org/cdn/brochures/mercedesbenz/2014-cclass.pdf . This is a reprint of the manufacturer brochure, not the hosting dealer's specification text.
- The brochure distinguishes the standard C63's twin bonnet power domes, grille/AMG apron, staggered wheels, four-door sedan roofline and rear exhaust arrangement from the Edition 507's vented hood. This mesh represents the standard facelift W204 sedan, not the 507 or coupe.
- Brochure dimensional table gives the C63 sedan 108.9-inch wheelbase and 186.0-inch overall length; it lists 79.1-inch width including mirrors. Geometry uses approximately 2.76m wheelbase and 4.71m bumper/tip envelope with a low sedan roof. These are scale guides, not a validated dimensional surface model.
- Added distinct sloping windscreen, separate side windows and pillars, four door handles/seams, tapered front/rear corners, actual wheel openings, broad projector-style lighting, grille star/louvre, lower apron openings and running lights, rear lamp surfaces, diffuser fins and paired exhaust outlets on each side.

## 2026 Honda HR-V, US market

- Honda, *2026 Honda HR-V Specifications & Features*: https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-6f8b202ddb1c7f26ecd92774bb014b0c-2026-honda-hr-v-specifications-and-features
- Honda, *2026 Honda HR-V Debuts New Styling and Tech Upgrades to Grow Popularity with Young Buyers*, with manufacturer exterior photos: https://hondanews.com/en-US/honda-automobiles/releases/2026-honda-hr-v-debuts-new-styling-and-tech-upgrades-to-grow-popularity-with-young-buyers
- Honda specifications give 104.5-inch wheelbase, 179.8-inch length, 72.4-inch width excluding mirrors, 63.4/63.8-inch height depending on trim, and LED headlights/daytime running lights. Wheel sizes, grille treatment and other equipment vary by trim.
- Mesh follows the US second-generation crossover's sloping roof/hatch, rear quarter windows, black window pillars, honeycomb-style grille and Honda badge, slim angled lights, arch/rocker cladding and high hatch spoiler. It does not silently select a Sport/EX-L equipment package. Mirror and optic details are simplified.

## Limits

The meshes establish recognisable proportions, separate inspectable body/glass/lamp surfaces and open wheel arches. They are not manufacturer CAD. Small internal optical elements, sheet-metal thicknesses, panel tolerances, fasteners and trim-specific details are not reconstructed. Wheel geometry and engine-bay geometry are supplied separately by the renderer and engine modules. Dimensions are approximate and must not be used to fabricate or fit parts.

## Panel and wheel revision

The W204's front and rear doors separate independently on either side. The
mirror and pane stay with each door. Its grille carries the single silver bar
and star, the bonnet carries the raised domes, and its rear lenses turn around
the quarter in successive vertical sections. The 2014 US brochure, under
“Wheel choices”, identifies the C63's standard 18-inch twin five-spoke wheel.
The optional single five-spoke and Edition 507 wheels are different.

Honda's photographs of the 2026 US HR-V show the narrow lamp units, honeycomb
front opening and small badge above the grille. The rear window, brake light,
spoiler and wiper travel with the hatch. The wheel face here illustrates the
LX's 17-inch cast wheel. The Sport and EX-L have distinct 18-inch wheels.
See [Honda's wheel photographs by trim](https://www.hondainfocenter.com/2026/HR-V/Feature-Guide/Exterior-Features/Wheels-and-Tires/).
