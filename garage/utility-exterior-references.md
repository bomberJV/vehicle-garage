# Utility exterior reference notes

Updated November 13, 2026.

The two exterior meshes in `exterior-utility.js` are original inspection geometry. They reconstruct visible panel relationships and characteristic shapes; they are not manufacturer CAD and have no factory dimensional tolerance. Bright trim, paint swatches, optional rack, mirrors and rear-window division are visual choices, not an assertion of original equipment on a particular VIN.

## 1993 Ford F-350 regular-cab / long Styleside box

- Ford, [1993 F-Series sales brochure](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/1993-Ford-F-Series.pdf), manufacturer brochure scanned by a third-party archive. Web index exposes the original Ford text and trim/cab options. Direct web-tool PDF extraction is limited by the15MB file size.
- Ford, [1993 commercial pickups and chassis brochure](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/1993-Ford-Commercial-Trucks-Pickups-Chassis.pdf), scanned manufacturer brochure. Catalog/configuration reference only; no engine or axle is selected here.
- Ford’s [1993 commercial pickups and chassis brochure](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/1993-Ford-Commercial-Trucks-Pickups-Chassis.pdf) lists a 133-inch wheelbase, 213.3-inch overall length and eight-foot box for the regular-cab F-350. The wheel centers and body arch centers use the 133-inch wheelbase.

Changes: separate front wings, hood/cowl, short two-door regular cab and long box; wheel openings cut into both front wings and bed sides; raised moulding and two-tone bright strip; twin front wipers; mirror support rods; regular-cab rear glazing; open ribbed cargo floor, wheel tubs, top rails/stake-pocket recesses; separate tailgate, latch, oval, tail lamps and chrome step bumper. The front uses the OBS composite rectangular headlamps with lower amber marker units, dark grille cavity, bright crossbars and central blue oval.

The single-rear-wheel illustration, mirror style, bright trim and sliding rear-window divisions do not establish a particular actual truck’s factory specification. Engine, transmission, 2WD/4WD, payload, axle and dual-rear-wheel options remain unresolved. The drawing must not be used to infer those options or mechanical fitment.

The regular cab now has two movable door assemblies with attached door glass, handles and mirrors. A stationary bench and dash are visible when the doors move outward. The tailgate separates behind the box, exposing the ribbed floor between the rear corner lamps. The narrow triangular vent pane sits forward of the main door glass.

The Mercedes-Benz parts listing for the [2002 G500's 7.5J × 18 five-spoke wheel](https://parts.mercedesbenzofmiami.com/v-2002-mercedes-benz-g500--base--5-0l-v8-gas/suspension--wheels) and [photos of the original G500 rims](https://www.originalwheels.com/mercedes-wheels/gclass2002rims.php) informed the tapered spoke faces, center cap and five mounting positions. The [1993 F-350 wheel catalog and photos](https://www.originalwheels.com/ford-wheels/trucks-f350rims-1993.php) identify the 16 × 7 single-rear-wheel steel option, with four slots and eight lugs. That wheel is shown on the regular-cab illustration.

### U.S. G500 exterior lighting

The [2002 G-Class Operator’s Manual](https://cdn.dealereprocess.org/cdn/servicemanuals/mercedesbenz/2002-gclass.pdf#page=331) identifies the bulb families on printed p. 260 and illustrates the mountings on pp. 332–341. On the [2002 G500 exterior photographs](https://www.edmunds.com/mercedes-benz/g-class/2002/pictures/) the amber markers sit below the wing rub strips ahead of the front arches. The red markers sit on the rear quarters behind the rear arches. The small clear repeater is on the upper side of the front wing near the door shut line. The rectangular front fogs use separate dark surrounds. The left rear bumper lens is red and the reverse lamp on the right is clear. The outer chamber of each upper tail lamp is pale beside the red chambers.

The [front fog parts plate](https://parts.mercedesbenzofmiami.com/v-2002-mercedes-benz-g500--base--5-0l-v8-gas/electrical--fog-lamps), [side marker plate](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/parts-list/2002-mercedes_benz-g500/headlights-lighting/side-marker-lamps), and [side repeater plate](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/parts-list/2002-mercedes_benz-g500/headlights-lighting/side-repeater-lamps) distinguish the housings, reflector, seal and service hardware. The bulb, socket, dust cover and spring-clip geometry follows the service illustrations. Lens pitches, optical prescriptions, mounting dimensions and artwork are illustrative.

The [early-series grille plate](https://classicparts.mbusa.com/v-2002-mercedes-benz-g500--base--5-0l-v8-gas/body--grille-and-components), [front fender trim plate](https://classicparts.mbusa.com/v-2002-mercedes-benz-g500--base--5-0l-v8-gas/body--exterior-trim-fender) and factory photographs of the U.S. 2002 model establish the early narrow grille vanes, inset perimeter above the bumper, separate painted headlamp surrounds and horizontal wing trim. Photographs also locate the small slanted louver near the front door and show how the fender extensions flatten at the crown. The bumper’s rounded corner and lower lip leave the rectangular fog-light openings on its flat central portion. Curvatures and flare sections are reconstructed from these views.

### G500 vehicle and workbench views

In the installed G500 coordinate frame, the grille is at −X and the US driver is at −Y. The front preset therefore puts the brake-fluid reservoir on the viewer's right and the expansion tank on the viewer's left. From behind, the red bumper fog lamp appears on the left and the clear backup lamp on the right. The side and default three-quarter views show the driver's side with the grille to the left. Picking, depth and drag translation use this same frame when inspecting an assembly in vehicle coordinates. A detached lamp, differential or other component uses its own fixture axes and its authored inspection angle.
